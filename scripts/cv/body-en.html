Hexagonal analytics: dropping the vendor without touching the domain

Architecture
Problem
Analytics on the main frontend was tied to an external vendor, and that dependency worked its way into the product code, which is exactly where a third party must never reach.
Approach
Full hexagonal discipline: a repository interface that defines what the domain needs, a storage manager behind it, and a transport layer with a circuit breaker and retry so a vendor outage can neither drag the interface down nor lose events.
Result
When the call came to drop the vendor, the adapter was swapped and the domain was left untouched: zero changes. Ports and adapters demonstrated in production instead of on a whiteboard.
